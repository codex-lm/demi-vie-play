# Demi-Vie

Demi-Vie is a side-view survival city builder. Twelve survivors seal a hatch and carve a shelter into the rock. They manage air, water, food, and heat while the radiation outside halves, again and again, until the Exodus.

Serve `index.html` over HTTP and open it in a browser. The game starts in the language of the browser, English or French; the other one is available in Options, with three interface palettes. Use the mouse wheel or two fingers to zoom, and the A− and A+ controls to change the interface size.
